Chapter 1 · Today assumed

The numbers exist. Getting them is the hard part.

  • Days, not minutesThe monthly report is copied together by hand.
  • Which number is right?Teams count things like "active user" differently.
  • No long memoryHistory is scattered, so "what was it 3 years ago?" takes digging.
  • Outages found lateWithout a live view, problems surface through complaints.

Chapter 4 · Further ahead

The rest of the Microsoft tenancy builds on the same data.

Once the lake and its definitions exist, every Microsoft tool we already have can use them.

OneLakeand the semantic model
  • CopilotAsk in plain English
  • TeamsAlerts and reports where people talk
  • ExcelAnalyse with live, governed data
  • Power AppsSmall apps to act on what you see
  • Power AutomateFollow-up tasks run themselves
  • ActivatorWatches the data for problems
  • PurviewLabels and protects sensitive data
  • Entra IDWho can see what

Illustrative: a leader asks Copilot in Teams

How many transactions did people complete in the app last month, compared with three years ago?

The answer comes from the same measure as the monthly pack, and Copilot only sees what the person asking is allowed to see.

Before anything else

Four things to check first.

  • Security clearanceIRAP covers Azure and Microsoft 365. Check Fabric and Power Platform scope.
  • PrivacyA privacy impact assessment before joining identity data. Counts and rates only.
  • HistoryThree-year comparisons need three years of data, kept the same way.
  • OwnershipEach source and each measure needs a business owner.
